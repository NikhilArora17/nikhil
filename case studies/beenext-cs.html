Discovery & Research03 / 10
Discovery
Understanding the CI's digital journey.
Custom Integrators research distribution partners the same way they evaluate premium brands — they look for authority, portfolio depth, and evidence of support infrastructure. The website had to answer three questions in under 8 seconds: Who are you? What brands do you carry? Why should I trust you?
Problem 01
Existing distributor websites in India look like product catalogues — dense, utilitarian, and indistinguishable. CIs have no emotional reason to prefer one over another.
Solution 01
Adopt a luxury editorial visual language: dark backgrounds, gold accents, generous whitespace, and architectural grid layouts. Make BEENEXT feel like a premium partner, not a warehouse.
Problem 02
Brand portfolio is BEENEXT's core differentiator, but most distributors bury it in navigation. CIs can't discover exclusive brands quickly enough.
Solution 02
Surface brands two ways: an auto-scrolling marquee for immediate recognition, and a bento-grid Featured Brands section for deeper discovery. Brand names appear within 2 scrolls.
Problem 03
The "Become a Partner" CTA — the primary business conversion — had no persistent visibility. Users left without being reminded of the next step.
Solution 03
Anchor "Become a Partner" in the navigation as a persistent gold button. Reinforce with a full-width Partner CTA section above the footer. Two guaranteed exposures per visit.
Competitor Benchmarking — Key Findings
Visual Identity
Generic, template-driven
90% of Indian VAD websites use blue/white templates with stock photography. Zero visual differentiation at a glance.
Navigation
Buried brand portfolios
Brand sections averaged 3 clicks deep. Logo recognition was inconsistent and missing on key landing pages.
Conversion
No visible CTA architecture
Partner CTAs appeared once, mid-page. No persistent nav CTA. Multi-field forms created high enquiry friction.
"If BEENEXT looked like every other distributor, Custom Integrators would treat it like every other distributor — as a commodity. The design needed to make the brand self-evident as the premium choice before a single word was read."

Typography System — Barlow Family
Headlines.
Barlow 800 — Display / Hero
Tight tracking −0.03em signals precision and authority. 800 weight at large size creates the luxury editorial impression at first glance.
Body copy flows with clarity and restraint — designed for sustained reading on dark backgrounds without eye fatigue.
Barlow 300 — Body copy
300 weight body paired with 800 headings creates stark contrast hierarchy. Single-family approach eliminates font-swap jank on load.
Section Colour Cadence
Core Design Principles
Principle 01
Gold as signal, not decoration
Every gold element earns its place: CTAs, borders, stats, tags. Gold is never used for body text or backgrounds. Restraint amplifies impact.
Principle 02
Architectural sharpness
Zero border radius throughout. Sharp card edges signal precision — consistent with enterprise technology positioning. Rounded corners feel consumer.
Principle 03
Strategic whitespace
Sections breathe. Generous padding signals premium positioning — crowded layouts signal commodity. Space is the luxury signal.

Animation & Interaction Design09 / 10
Interaction Design
Motion as brand language.
Entrance Animations
Hero Load Sequence
Logo + eyebrow (0ms) → H1 line 1 (300ms) → H1 line 2 (450ms) → H1 gold word (600ms) → sub-copy (700ms) → CTA primary (900ms) → CTA secondary (1050ms)
Scroll Entrance
IntersectionObserver: translateY(20px) → Y(0) + opacity(0→1) over 600ms ease-out. Section items stagger at 100ms increments.
Brand Marquee
Pure CSS @keyframes infinite scroll at 30s linear. animation-play-state:paused on hover. No JS required.
Hover Micro-Interactions
Cards
200ms ease
border-color: rgba(255,187,80,0.12) → rgba(255,187,80,0.5). Subtle gold glow: box-shadow:0 0 20px rgba(255,187,80,0.07).
Navigation Links
200ms ease
color → #FFBB50 + ::after pseudo thin bottom border slides in from left (scaleX 0→1, transform-origin:left).
CTA Buttons
200ms ease
Gold filled: background slides to #E79D29 (marigold) + scale(1.02). Outlined: white fill at 8% opacity on hover.
Admin Config Panel — config.html
Architecture
Password-protected localStorage CMS
SHA-256 hashed key comparison (client-side). 3-attempt lockout with 30s cooldown. Hero, brands, stats, blog, contact — all editable without touching code.
UX Decision
Dark-themed, brand-consistent admin UI
Config panel matches site visual language — not a jarring light-mode admin break. Collapsible sections reduce cognitive load. JSON export as backup.
