Discovery & Brand System

Discovery

Brand principles that drove
every decision.

Principle 01

Premium Restraint

Every element earns its place. Remove what doesn't communicate. The result should feel curated — not busy.

Principle 02

Motion with Purpose

Animation is not decoration. Every transition must convey state, guide attention, or reinforce brand energy. Nothing moves for movement's sake.

Principle 03

Mobile-First Craft

The first visit happens on a phone. Design decisions begin at 390px and scale up — never the reverse.

Why Work Is Section #3

Proof before pitch

Showing work before explaining services bypasses the credibility question. The visitor validates quality in their first 8 seconds — then is primed to read how you do it.

Fixed Navigation + Pill CTA

Text links navigate, buttons act

"Start a project" is the only pill button in the nav — all other items are navigation links. Always visible. Nav gains blur background on scroll.

Work Section — Auto-Scroll Marquee

Show more without asking for a click

A horizontal auto-scroll marquee surfaces the entire portfolio passively. Cards are 9:16 — Instagram-native ratio. RAF-cancelled when off-screen.

Primary Conversion Path

Hero → Work → Contact

Average 3 scroll stops from landing to conversion action. Services and Process support credibility — they are not the path.

Designing for Constraints

Design for Constraints

When limitations made
the design better.

Four moments during the project where a technical or device constraint forced a design rethink — and in each case, the constraint produced a more considered result.

Animation Audit

The hero had 50 animated elements competing for attention — and was crashing iPhones. We treated animation like typography: only what communicates energy earns its place. We reduced, organised, and only kept motion that reinforced the brand's kinetic character.

Ghost Button Duplication

Animating the whole button broke the wave-strip illusion. Solution: only the arrow moves — a directed gesture, not a floating shape.

Texture Rendering Artefacts

A grain filter forced one compositor layer and flickered on Android. Removed entirely — cleaner, more legible, better performance.

Glass Blur at Scale

Backdrop-blur on 30+ cards loaded the GPU and read as cluttered. Replaced with solid rgba() overlay — visually identical, zero cost.

Outcomes & Reflection

Outcomes

What shipped and what it taught us.

Premium Positioning

Reads like a top-tier agency

Dark/blue rhythm, tight type, and wave motion consistently draw comparisons to international agencies — from an Indian market client.

Mobile Performance

60fps on iPhone 12+

Despite a complex animation system, every decision was validated on device first. The constraint audit paid off.

Proof of work is the primary conversion driver — not the about page. The IA decision to front-load the portfolio held up in testing.
