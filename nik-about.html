A bit about me.

I'm a UX/UI Designer with a background in Game Arts, bringing together visual thinking, creativity, and problem-solving to design intuitive and engaging digital experiences.

My background in design has taught me to think about both how something looks and, more importantly, how it works and feels for the people using it. I enjoy understanding problems, exploring ideas, and turning them into clear, thoughtful interfaces.

I'm naturally curious, always learning, and excited by opportunities to create meaningful products while growing as a UX/UI Designer.
